Questions

Before you enrol

Do I need any experience with AI tools?

No. Module 1 starts from an empty screen and every step is shown on video.

What equipment do I need?

A laptop and a phone. The image and video tools run in the cloud; expect to spend a modest amount each month on them, and we tell you which ones are worth it.

Do I have to say the creator is AI?

Yes. Fanvue and the social platforms require AI-generated creators to be labelled, and we build that in from the start. Accounts that hide it get removed.

How much will I earn?

We can’t tell you, and you should be wary of anyone who does. The course gives you the method and the metrics; results depend on your niche, consistency and time.

How long does it take?

Most of the setup — model, accounts, profile — fits into a few focused weeks. Growing an audience takes longer and never really stops.

Is the adult content module compulsory?

No. Plenty of AI creators stay entirely in fashion, fitness or lifestyle. Module 7 is there for those who want it, for adults only.
